## 👋 About Me

I'm an Economics &amp; Data Science undergraduate at COMSATS University Islamabad,
working at the intersection of **applied econometrics, machine learning, and
MLOps**. Most of my work models volatility, herding behavior, and forecasting
problems in Pakistani financial markets and macroeconomic data — and I'm
currently focused on taking that research from notebooks into production
systems.

- 🔭 **Currently researching:** herding &amp; volatility spillovers across
  KSE-100, gold, and crypto; ML-based remittance forecasting; economic policy
  uncertainty &amp; bank credit dynamics
- 🌱 **Currently learning:** MLOps — model deployment, pipelines, and
  monitoring, shared as a learning-in-public series on LinkedIn
- 🧠 **Core toolkit:** ARDL/ECM, NARDL, GARCH-family models,
  Diebold-Yilmaz connectedness, HMM regime-switching, XGBoost/Random Forest
  with SHAP interpretability
- 💬 **Ask me about:** time-series forecasting, volatility modeling, or
  making ML models interpretable
- 📫 **Reach me at:** sameerhassankhan6@gmail.com
